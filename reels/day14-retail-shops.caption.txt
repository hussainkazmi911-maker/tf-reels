Shop owners: suit dukaan mein bik gaya, aur website par ab bhi "available" hai. Phir customer order karta hai aur aap ko refund dena parta hai.

Forge Fix of the Day #14 - shop aur website ka stock ek system mein:
- Counter par sale hoti hai, website ka stock usi waqt update ho jata hai
- Online order aaye to shop ka stock bhi saath kam hota hai
- Ek screen par dikhta hai: kya bika, kya bacha
- Har order tracked: ordered, packed, delivered

Shop owner? Comment "STOCK" karein, hum aap ko details bhej denge.
Aap ka koi jaanne wala shop owner stock ke masle se pareshan hai? Ye reel unhein bhej dein.
